# Juniper Lane Retail: Store Replenishment and Markdown Policy (Demo)

> Synthetic policy for demonstration only. Not Juniper Lane's real policy and not a substitute for your own approved merchandising rules.

## 1. Stock cover targets

| Item type | Target weeks of cover | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Core basics (Home, Kitchen) | 3 to 6 weeks | Replenish on a standing cycle |
| Trend items (Apparel, Footwear) | 2 to 4 weeks | Shorter cover because styles change quickly |

Weeks of cover = on-hand units / average weekly units sold over the last 8 weeks.

## 2. Stock flags

| Flag | Rule | Required response |
|------|------|-------------------|
| Stockout risk | Weeks of cover below 2.0 | Check on-order ETA. Do not start a new markdown. Ask the regional team about a transfer. |
| OK | Weeks of cover from 2.0 to 8.0 | No action beyond the normal replenishment cycle. |
| Overstock risk | Weeks of cover above 8.0 | Transfer first (see section 3). Markdown only if transfer is not possible. |

## 3. Transfers before markdowns

1. Look for another store in the same region with weeks of cover below 4.0 for the same SKU.
2. A transfer is viable if the receiving store has weeks of cover below 4.0 and the sending store stays at or above 2.0 after the move.
3. Transfers must be completed within 14 days. If not, move to a markdown review.

## 4. Markdown rules

- Markdown depth is capped at 30% without Regional Merchandising Director approval.
- Markdowns above 25% need Regional Merchandising Director approval.
- Do not stack a markdown on a SKU that already has an active promotion at the same store. Note the conflict instead.
- Do not start a markdown on a SKU with stockout risk at that store.
- Markdown proposals must state the expected units to clear and the expected margin impact.

## 5. Supplier lead times

- Use the supplier lead time and on-time reliability in the supplier file.
- If an on-order delivery arrives after the item runs out, flag the gap and recommend an expedite request or a transfer.
- Reliability below 90% on time means add one extra week to any cover calculation.

## 6. Data freshness

- Inventory snapshots older than 7 days are stale. Say so and do not give a final recommendation without a refresh.
- Promotions with status "Ended" are not active. Promotions with status "Planned" are not active until their start date.

## 7. Human approval

- All outputs are recommendations. Store managers and merchandising leads confirm every action.
- Do not promise customers stock, delivery dates or discounts that are not in the approved data.
